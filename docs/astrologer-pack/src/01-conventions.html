Calculation conventions

Every rule the app applies, written out so it can be checked. Please mark anything that disagrees with your almanac — a cross in the margin is enough.

2 · The five limbs

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana are all reported as they stand at sunrise, together with the exact clock time at which each one ends and the name of the one that follows. End times are found to the second.

LimbRule used
TithiEach 12° of elongation of the Moon from the Sun. Paksha from the tithi number.
NakshatraEach 13°20′ of the Moon's sidereal longitude.
YogaEach 13°20′ of the sum of the Sun's and Moon's sidereal longitudes.
KaranaHalf a tithi, 6° of elongation.
Lunar monthBoth amanta and purnimanta are computed, with an adhika-masa flag. The app currently displays purnimanta. See question 4.
Vrat markedEkadashi, Purnima, Amavasya only. See question 9.
Eras shownBikram Sambat, Nepal Sambat (year and month), Shaka.

5 · Hora

Twelve equal parts of the daylight and twelve of the night, so a hora is only sixty minutes long at an equinox. The first hora after sunrise belongs to the lord of the weekday, and each following hora takes the next lord in Chaldean order:

Shani → Guru → Mangal → Surya → Shukra → Budha → Chandra → (repeating)

Twenty-four horas later the sequence arrives at the lord of the next weekday, which is the check we use to know the table is right.

Quality shownLords
AuspiciousGuru, Shukra, Budha, Chandra
FairSurya
InauspiciousShani, Mangal
Please check this classification. Almanacs differ over the Sun in particular, and some treat Budha as variable rather than benefic. See question 7.

6 · Planets and charts

Planetary positions shown on the panchang screen — Mangal, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu — are sidereal longitudes with the rashi, taken at sunrise. Rahu is the mean node, and Ketu is placed opposite it.

The birth chart is a separate, weaker calculation and is not yet trustworthy. It presently uses an older mean-longitude method accurate to roughly one degree, marks only Rahu and Ketu as retrograde, and uses whole-sign houses. We are replacing it with the same ephemeris the panchang uses. Please do not verify chart output from the current build — panchang output is ready for review, charts are not.
