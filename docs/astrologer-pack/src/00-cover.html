Panchang & Jyotish review pack

For the astrologers advising the Sanatan Nepal app. This pack sets out exactly how the app calculates what it shows, asks you to rule on the points where traditions differ, and lists the written content we need from you.

Why you are receiving this

The app computes panchang and chart values from an astronomical ephemeris. The arithmetic is reliable; the conventions are not ours to choose. Which ayanamsa, which sunrise, amanta or purnimanta, where Abhijit falls — these are decisions a tradition makes, and a printed panchang from Kathmandu, Varanasi and Chennai will disagree with one another on several of them.

We would rather be openly wrong in a way you have corrected than quietly wrong in a way nobody checked. Everything the app assumes is written down in document 2 so you can mark it right or wrong.

The three things we need back

  1. Corrections to document 2 — any rule that does not match your practice. Marking the page by hand and photographing it is perfectly fine.
  2. Answers to document 3. Where two traditions both have a claim, please say which one this app should follow, and whether the answer differs for Nepal and for India.
  3. The verification worksheet, filled in against a printed panchang you trust. Please name the almanac you used — that matters as much as the numbers.
On disagreement: if your almanac differs from ours by a few minutes on a tithi ending, that is usually a different ayanamsa or sunrise rule and is worth knowing about. If it differs by a whole tithi or nakshatra, something is wrong at our end and we want to hear about it quickly.

What the app does today

Live now: full panchang for any date (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana with their end times), sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset, Bikram Sambat and Nepal Sambat, Shaka, moon sign, planetary positions at sunrise, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Abhijit, the sixteen Choghadiya of day and night, and the twenty-four horas.

Built but awaiting your review before we trust it: birth chart, Vimshottari dasha, Mangal / Kaal Sarp / Sade Sati, and Ashtakoota matching.

Not built: numerology, Ashtakavarga, divisional charts, the Poruthams, and the regional (Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam) panchang variants.
