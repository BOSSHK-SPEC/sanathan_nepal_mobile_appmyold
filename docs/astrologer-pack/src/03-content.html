Written content we need from you

The app can calculate every number it needs. What it cannot produce is meaning — what a yoga signifies, what a dosha calls for, what a score implies. That writing has to come from an astrologer, and it is the work that will decide how soon these features can ship.

How to send it

A plain document or spreadsheet is ideal — one row or heading per item, with the text beneath. Nepali, Hindi and English are all needed; where you can only write one, English is the most useful because it can be translated and then reviewed by you.

Three rules for every passage. No absolute verdicts about a person's life, marriage, health or money. No medical, legal or financial instruction. Nothing that would frighten a reader who has no astrologer to ask — remedies should be practical and calming, not alarming.

Twelve signs across four periods. Today this text is written editorially. 6 · Numerology

Not built yet, and the cheapest feature on our list to build — the arithmetic is trivial. It is entirely blocked on content: which system (Chaldean or Pythagorean), and a passage for each number in each position. Please advise whether it is worth offering at all.

7 · Festivals and vrats

For each festival the app shows a name, a date, the tithi it falls on, how it is observed, and places associated with it. The Nepal and India lists exist but are thin. Corrections to dates and tithis matter most; descriptions second.

What we are not asking you for

Any calculation — positions, timings, dasha dates, koota scores. The app computes all of it. We need your judgement on the conventions, and your words for what the numbers mean.
